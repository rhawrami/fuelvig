Methodology

Here's how Fuel Vig works, and where we get our data.

01

Baseline

The treatment period begins on February 28, 2026. The starting counterfactual price is the arithmetic mean of all available observations from January 29 through February 27, 2026 for the selected state and grade.

Source coverage is uneven in this window. For example, only one observation is available for each of 42 jurisdictions. The calculator reports the number of baseline observations used so that this limitation remains visible.

02

Driver assumptions

Each fill-up is assumed to take a vehicle from one-quarter full to completely full. Gallons purchased per visit therefore equal 75 percent of the selected vehicle's reported tank capacity.

If matching engines share one tank capacity and fuel category, the calculator uses it automatically. If capacities or fuel categories differ, the driver must select an engine or variant. Missing and invalid capacities cannot produce an estimate.

03

Price averaging

If the user selects one monthly fill, then that month's arithmetic average is used. Two or more fills use four fixed periods: days 1-7, 8-14, 15-21, and 22 through month-end. Fills are distributed across those periods as evenly as possible, starting with the earliest period.

If a period has no observation, the month's available-observation average is used. Months with no observations are omitted.

For an incomplete current month, the estimate includes only fill periods that have begun by the latest available observation.

04

Counterfactual

At the default setting, the baseline price remains constant. A user-selected trend compounds monthly. For example, at a 5% trend, a baseline of $1.00 becomes $1.05 in month two, and $1.1025 in month three.

05

Calculation

gallons per fill = tank gallons x 0.75 monthly difference = sum(actual price - alternative price) x gallons per fill total difference = sum(monthly differences)

A positive result is additional estimated spending. A negative result is estimated savings relative to the selected counterfactual.

06

Limitations

  • Historical observations are not uniformly daily across dates or jurisdictions.
  • AAA values are jurisdiction averages, not prices at a driver's specific station.
  • Tank capacities are source-reported and currently unverified unless noted.
  • Make, model, and year can map to several overlapping generations or variants.

07

Sources

National and state prices come from AAA Gas Prices. Historical backfill was initialized from jacobschulman/gas-tracker. Vehicle specifications come from gor3a/vehicle-makes-models.
